# Memory Match

## ▶ [Play the game](https://libyan11.github.io/memory-match/)

https://libyan11.github.io/memory-match/

Flip cards and find the pairs, in your browser on phone or computer. Play online with friends, against computer players, or on your own to beat your best. Works offline after the first visit. Part of [Game Night](https://libyan11.github.io/).

**Play with friends:** one person taps *Create a room* and shares the 4-letter code or link. 2 to 6 players; the host can add computer players and pick the board size.

**Rules**
- Flip two cards. A pair is yours, and you go again.
- No pair: the cards turn back and the next player goes. Remember where they were!
- Most pairs wins. On your own: find them all in as few moves as you can.
- Boards: Small (8 pairs), Medium (12), Large (18).
